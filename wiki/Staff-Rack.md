# Staff Rack

The Staff Rack is a slim **wall-mounted** rail with **four** hooks. It stores Caszual MTG targeting staffs and displays their actual tall 3D models hanging vertically.

## Placement and appearance

- Right-click the **side face of a wall** to mount the rack; it faces outward automatically.
- Position the rack around **two blocks above the floor**. Staffs hang almost two blocks below the mount, with the tips near the hooks and the lower ends touching the ground.
- The wooden rail has four metal hooks across one block. The shafts are narrow enough to hang side by side without the amethyst heads clipping.
- The hanging staffs are visuals. Their storage and interaction are handled by the wall rack, not the empty space below it.

## Using the rack

- Hold an **MTG Staff** and right-click the rack: transfers one staff to the first empty hook.
- Right-click with an empty hand or another item to open the **four-slot Staff Rack inventory**.
- Use its four slots to remove, move, or shift-click staffs in/out.
- Other items are not accepted.

Each staff retains all its item data, including its own RGB left-click and right-click outline colors.

## Breaking and moving

Mining the rack drops **one Staff Rack item retaining all four staffs**. Reinstall it on a wall to restore its contents and slot positions. It does not drop individual staffs separately when mined.

## Crafting

```text
Iron Nugget   Iron Bars  Iron Nugget
Dark Oak      (empty)    Dark Oak
```

Uses two dark oak planks, two iron nuggets, and iron bars.
