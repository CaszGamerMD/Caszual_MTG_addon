# Storage and Multiplayer

## Shared collections

Land, Token and Community Card data are server/world data, not tied to a particular database block.

- Replacing or breaking a database block does not erase the collection.
- Multiple copies of the same database type access the same shared data.
- The data is shared across dimensions through the world's saved data.
- Players currently share access; there is no per-player ownership/permission layer for the community databases.

## Lands and tokens

Land and Token databases store catalogue templates, not finite stock.

Once a land/token printing is known, players can request free copies without depleting a quantity.

## Community cards

Regular Community Card entries have a real count.

- deposits increase shared stock;
- inventory/deckbox withdrawals consume stock;
- Deck Builder assembly consumes regular Community Card stock;
- lands and tokens generated by their catalogue types do not consume stock.

## Card Hand privacy

The Hand is different from community storage:

- card contents live in that specific Hand block entity;
- the owner controls the viewer list;
- actual card stacks are sent only to authorized viewers unless reveal mode is enabled;
- the public podium model only reveals an approximate/count-state facedown visual, never card identities.

## Custom Deckbox

Custom Deckbox card storage remains MTGCard deckbox storage. Caszual MTG stores the selected exterior material alongside it.

## Backups

The world contains the community collection/catalogue saved data, while MTGCard manages its own related card/deckbox data. Normal server/world backups are recommended.

## Deck Builder saved drafts and output

Each Deck Builder block retains its last imported decklist, URL, resolved card rows, missing-card list and commander setting. Background lookups can finish after the screen closes. Successfully resolved Archidekt imports are cached for six hours. Built cards go into an MTGCard deckbox **item in the Builder's own GUI slot**; the inserted deckbox drops if the Builder is mined.

## Deckbox Rack

The wall/floor mounted Deckbox Rack stores five MTGCard deckbox item stacks as part of the rack. Its actual deckbox item models are displayed as miniatures. Breaking the rack returns a rack item retaining all five deckboxes and their MTGCard storage references.

## Card Store output

The Card Store has an additional deckbox slot saved on its block entity. Purchased cards route into the inserted deckbox first. If full, the store tries free buyer inventory slots; otherwise MTGCard's normal neighboring deckbox/front ejection logic remains. Breaking the Card Store drops its inserted deckbox.
